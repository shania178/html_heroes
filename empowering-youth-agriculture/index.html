OUR MISSION

Building Sustainable Future for Ghana's Youth

Through our 3-month agribusiness incubation program, we hope to train and support 40 youth (50% women) to establish viable agricultural livelihoods that generate income, strengthen food security, and reduce migration pressure on Accra.
