About the Program

Heroes is a youth-led social enterprise that addresses unemployment and rural-urban migration in Ghana by equipping young people in Northern Ghana with market-linked, climate-resilient agribusiness skills through a 3-month incubation program.
The program runs for 3 months. Each month focuses on a different phase: Foundation (farming fundamentals), Development (agribusiness skills), and Launch (market access and starter kits).
Our 5 core components are: (1) Climate-Resilient Farming, (2) Agribusiness & Entrepreneurship, (3) Starter Inputs & Demo Farms, (4) Mentorship & Cooperatives, and (5) Market Linkages. Each component is designed to build practical skills and long-term livelihoods.
No, the program is completely free for selected participants. All training, materials, starter kits, and mentorship are funded through partnerships and supporters.

Eligibility & Application

The program is open to youth aged 18–35 in Northern Ghana who are unemployed or underemployed and interested in building a career in agriculture. No prior farming experience is required.
You can apply by reaching out through our contact page or visiting one of our community outreach sessions. Our team will guide you through the application process and answer any questions you have.
Each cohort trains 40 youth, with a target of 50% women participants. This ensures a balanced and inclusive learning environment.

After the Program

Graduates receive a starter kit containing seeds, tools, and basic irrigation equipment. They also gain access to mentorship, cooperative farming groups, and market connections.
