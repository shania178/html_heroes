Our Mission & Vision

Transforming youth unemployment through sustainable agribusiness

Mission Statement

Heroes is a youth-led social enterprise addressing youth unemployment and rural–urban migration in Ghana by equipping young people in Northern Ghana with market-linked, climate-resilient agribusiness skills.

Through a 3-month agribusiness incubation program, we will train and support 40 youth (50% women) to establish viable agricultural livelihoods that generate income, strengthen food security, and reduce migration pressure on Accra.

Unlike conventional training-only interventions, Heroes combines hands-on training, starter inputs, mentorship, cooperative farming models, and market linkages to ensure participants transition from skills acquisition to sustained income generation.

The Challenge

Problem Statement

Youth unemployment in Ghana remains a critical development challenge with far-reaching consequences.


34.4% Youth Unemployment

According to Ghana Statistical Service (2025), over a third of young people aged 15-24 are unemployed.

1.3 Million NEET

Young people Not in Employment, Education, or Training, representing a massive untapped potential.

Rural-Urban Migration

Northern Ghana youth migrate to Accra seeking scarce urban jobs while rural agricultural potential remains underutilized.

Agricultural Challenges

Limited opportunities, reliance on rain-fed farming, low adoption of modern techniques, and negative perceptions of agriculture.

Our Approach

The Solution

Heroes Agribusiness Incubation Model: A 3-month, practice-driven program focused on dry-season, resilient and market-oriented agriculture.


1

Modern, Climate-Resilient Agriculture Training

  • Crop production (vegetables, grains)
  • Dry-season irrigation techniques
  • Poultry and small livestock
  • Sustainable soil and water management
2

Agribusiness & Entrepreneurship Skills

  • Farm budgeting and record-keeping
  • Value addition and post-harvest handling
  • Cooperative models and collective marketing
3

Starter Inputs & Demonstration Farms

  • Seeds, tools, and basic equipment
  • Community demonstration plots
