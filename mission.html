OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

Our mission is to reduce youth unemployment in Ghana by empowering young people with practical skills, entrepreneurial mindsets, and access to economic opportunities, enabling them to build sustainable livelihoods, contribute to national development, and reduce rural–urban migration.

PROGRAM STRUCTURE

3-Month Incubation Phases

Each phase builds on the last — from foundational skills to market-ready graduates.

Month 2 Development Agribusiness training, cooperative models, farm budgeting, record-keeping, value addition Business mindset & planning ready
Month 3 Launch Market access strategies, starter kit handover, mentorship pairing, demo farm setup Graduates equipped to earn

Program Details

Phase 1 — Foundation (Month 1)

Participants are introduced to the fundamentals of modern agriculture and sustainable farming practices tailored to Northern Ghana's climate conditions.

  • Land preparation and soil health assessment
  • Seed selection, planting techniques, and crop spacing
  • Dry-season irrigation methods (drip, sprinkler, canal)
  • Water conservation and harvesting strategies
  • Introduction to climate-resilient crop varieties
  • Pest and disease identification and organic control
  • Hands-on practice on demonstration plots

Phase 2 — Development (Month 2)

The focus shifts from field skills to business and cooperative skills, preparing participants to treat farming as a viable enterprise.

  • Farm budgeting, cost estimation, and financial planning
  • Record-keeping and basic bookkeeping for small farms
  • Value addition techniques (processing, packaging, storage)
  • Post-harvest handling to reduce losses and improve quality
  • Cooperative formation and collective decision-making
  • Group marketing and negotiation with buyers
  • Guest sessions with successful local agribusiness owners

Phase 3 — Launch (Month 3)

The final phase equips graduates with the tools, connections, and confidence to start generating income independently.

  • Starter kit distribution (seeds, tools, irrigation equipment)
  • Community demonstration plot setup and management
  • Market linkage sessions with local buyers and traders
  • Mentor pairing with experienced farmers and business owners
  • Business plan development and pitch practice
  • Graduation ceremony and alumni network induction
  • Post-program follow-up schedule established

Post-Program Support

Graduates are not left on their own after the 3 months. Ongoing support ensures sustained success and long-term livelihood growth.

  • Monthly mentor check-ins for the first 6 months
  • Access to cooperative farming groups and shared resources
  • Continued market connections and buyer introductions
  • Opportunity to rejoin future cohorts as peer mentors
  • Access to microfinance and savings group referrals
